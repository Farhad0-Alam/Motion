# Outgrown (realtor white-label promo)

30 s, 5 shots × 6 s, wordless. A hermit crab outgrows its shell and has to find a
home before a circling gull lands; an octopus real estate agent saves the day.
Built for realtor clients: the last 3 s carry each agent's own end card.
Written 2026-10-19; not scheduled.

| Time | Shot | Beat | Hook role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | 1 | Tiny shell rattles and bursts; crab exposed, blushes, hides behind a leaf; gull shadow sweeps over | Frame-1 chaos + ticking clock |
| 6–12 | 2 | Viewing #1: giant conch; walks in, echoes, slides out the back | Comedy |
| 12–18 | 3 | Viewing #2: perfect shell, but a grumpy snail lives there and slams its door; gull lands nearby | Stakes rise |
| 18–24 | 4 | Octopus agent fans out 8 shells; crab dives into the right one just as the beak strikes: BONK | Payoff of the clock |
| 24–30 | 5 | Peeks out, welcome mat, octopus hands over a key; gull sulks off; end card 27–30 | Resolution + CTA |

## Characters
```
SHELBY (hermit crab): small, round coral-orange cartoon hermit crab, soft rounded shapes, huge glossy black eyes on short stalks, expressive brows, one big claw and one small claw, tiny legs.
OTTO (octopus real estate agent): friendly lavender-purple octopus, big kind eyes, tiny navy bow tie, holds a clipboard and a ring of brass keys.
GULL: big scruffy white-and-grey seagull with a beady yellow eye.
SETTING: sunny tropical beach, wet golden sand at the tide line, small dark rocks, scattered seashells, turquoise water, soft white foam, warm late-morning sun.
STYLE: high-end stylized 3D animated feature film, soft rounded cartoon characters, strong squash-and-stretch, shallow depth of field. Not photorealistic. Vertical 9:16.
```

A large curved dried leaf on the sand next to it. No text.
