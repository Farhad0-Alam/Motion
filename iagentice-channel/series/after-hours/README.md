# After Hours (ClientFalcon Voice)

Wordless 3D cartoon, 20–30 s. Same story spine, new industry, set and joke each time.

## Recurring cast (every episode)
- **Doug**: the owner, a huge sleepy brown bear; a different trade each episode.
- **Ember**: the coral dot from the ClientFalcon logo, alive. Lives in Doug's website chat (or WhatsApp), takes the customer's details, carries them to Doug as an envelope (the email) and waits by his phone until 7 AM. Never answers a phone, books or fixes anything.
- **Rex**: the rival, a smug raccoon in a teal bomber jacket. Always misses the call, always arrives too late.
- One guest customer per episode with that night's disaster.

## Story spine
1. **Hook:** a customer's night-time emergency, already in full chaos. Clock in Night `#9385FF` (e.g. 2:47 AM).
2. **Ticking clock:** they message three businesses. Two are dead (closed sign, voicemail beep); the competitor's lights are on.
3. **Payoff:** the third business's website chat/voice bubble answers at once and takes their details. 7:00 AM (Dawn `#F7A63B`): the owner wakes to a "New lead" email, calls back, wins the job.
4. **End card (added in edit):** *Work that never sleeps.* · ClientFalcon mark · demo link.
