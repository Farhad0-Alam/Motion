## Edit notes
- Keep the cheek size consistent within a shot. It's allowed to grow between shots 1, 2 and 3 (that's the fuse), and must be normal in shot 5 until the walnuts go in.
- The muffled "mmf-mmmf" is the model's job. If a take gives real words, mute that span and add a synthesized mumble in post.
