## Logline
At 11:38 PM, Pip the chipmunk's cheek swells up like a balloon. The dental office across the street switches its sign off, and a call to another dentist hits voicemail. Pip can't talk anyway, so he types into Dr. Hilda's website chat, which answers and takes his details. At 7:00 AM Dr. Hilda reads the "New lead" email mid-toothbrush and calls him.

## What makes this one different
The spine's twist here is **"the office is closed, the website isn't."** The tooth-shaped sign switching off belongs to Dr. Hilda's own office, and her website chat still answers. The other dead end is a voicemail he can't even speak into.

## Cast (new pair for this trade)
- **Pip**, the customer: a small chipmunk in a mint nightshirt whose right cheek balloons bigger in every shot. The swelling is the visible fuse.
- **Dr. Hilda**, the owner: a big, gentle hippo dentist with tortoiseshell glasses, the whitest teeth in town and a giant toothbrush.

## Set
Pip's one-room home inside a hollow tree (tree-ring walls, acorn jars, a round window looking across the street at the dental office's tooth sign), then Dr. Hilda's pink-tiled bathroom at dawn, then the front step of her office in morning sun.

## The joke
- **Hook:** the frozen peas on Pip's cheek melt in one second, and the heavy cheek tips him off the bed.
- **Middle:** he finally gets a voicemail, and all that comes out is "mmf-mmmf."
- **Payoff:** cheek back to normal, Pip celebrates by stuffing both cheeks with walnuts. Dr. Hilda does a horrified double-take, then laughs.

## Product beat
1. Dead ends: the office sign goes dark, and the call hits voicemail.
2. Dr. Hilda's **website chat** answers at once. Pip can type even though he can't talk. It takes his name and number and says it has sent them to Dr. Hilda.
3. At 7:00 AM: **"New lead: Pip (Hilda Dental)"** from ClientFalcon Voice. She calls him herself, and he's in her office that morning.

## Honesty check (plan/rules.md)
- [x] Website chat only, never a phone call. The voicemail belongs to a different dentist.
- [x] No booking: the chat takes details. Dr. Hilda arranges the visit on her callback.
- [x] **No medical advice on screen.** The chat says nothing about cold packs, painkillers or "come in at 8". The frozen peas are Pip's own idea, played as a gag, not advice.
- [x] The email overlay matches the real lead alert format.
- [x] No stats or reviews. Phone number in 555-01xx.
