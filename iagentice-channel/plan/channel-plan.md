# Channel plan

## Goal
Shorts that make a business owner in a specific industry think "that's my 2 AM",
then click through to the ClientFalcon demo (or imknownas for cards). Views matter
less than **demo clicks per industry**; the playlists double as sales material
for outreach ("here's our dentist video").

## Audience
Owners and managers of local service businesses, US-first:
plumber, dentist, HVAC, realtor, locksmith, auto repair, barber/salon,
restaurant/catering, roofer. Later: photographers, wedding planners, food trucks
(imknownas).

## Formats
| Format | Product | Share of uploads | Built with |
|---|---|---|---|
| [After Hours](../series/after-hours/README.md) | ClientFalcon | 2 per week | Omni/Veo shots + sound design in `videos/` |
| [Card Graveyard](../series/card-graveyard/README.md) | imknownas | 1 every other week | Omni/Veo shots |
| [Missed-Lead Math](../series/missed-lead-math/README.md) | ClientFalcon | 1 every other week | HyperFrames (this repo) |
| [Real Demos](../series/real-demos/README.md) | ClientFalcon | as available | Screen recording of the live demo |

## Cadence and distribution
- 3 uploads a week; the same vertical file goes to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook.
- One playlist per industry, plus one per series.
- Title pattern speaks to the owner: *"Plumbers, this is your 2 AM"*, *"Your dentist was asleep. This wasn't."*
- First frame carries the hook on Shorts; the title matters more on long-form and search.

## What to measure (review every 2 weeks)
- Per industry: views, average % viewed, swipe-away rate in the first 3 s, demo-link clicks.
- After 6 weeks, the industry with the most demo clicks gets a mini-season (4–6 episodes).
- Kill or rework any format whose first-3-second swipe-away stays worst for 3 uploads in a row.

## Long-form
Every ~15 Shorts, join them into an 8–10 minute industry compilation
("After Hours: every trade, one night") for mid-roll eligibility and search.
