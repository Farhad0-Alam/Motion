# Rules

## Product honesty (same rules as the websites)
- **Never show ClientFalcon answering a phone call.** The phone line is not live. Show the website chat/voice bubble or WhatsApp.
- **Never show booking.** It takes the customer's details and emails the owner; that is the payoff.
- Dentist, HVAC and similar agents capture details; they never give medical or repair advice on screen.
- imknownas: show only shipped features (digital card, NFC tap, save contact). Voice cloning, voice booking, checkout in chat, lead qualification, multi-agent routing are *early access*; don't show them as working.
- No invented statistics, customer counts or reviews. Missed-Lead Math numbers are labelled "example".
- Demo recordings are labelled "Demo".

## YouTube policy guardrails
- **Inauthentic content:** every episode gets its own story, set and joke. Never batch near-identical videos with only the industry name swapped.
- **Made for kids:** these are cartoons, so aim the humor at adult business owners (work, money, customers) and set the audience honestly at upload.
- **Altered or synthetic content label:** needed only for realistic-looking footage; the cartoon style usually doesn't need it. Tick it when unsure.
- No copyrighted characters or lookalikes (the reference sample used Angry Birds-style hatchlings; we design our own).
