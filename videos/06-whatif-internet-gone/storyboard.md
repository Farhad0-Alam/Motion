# Storyboard: What if the Internet Disappeared for a Year? (animated)

The story is the narration in `scenes.txt` (lines 001–093, ~8:04). This storyboard turns it into **38 animated
shots on 9 reusable sets**. Each line says what the characters *do* while the narrator speaks it. Line numbers are the
`scenes.txt` ids; times are estimates from word count and get re-timed to the real voice clips.

Rules for every shot
- One clear action per narration line. Lead the eye to where it happens, play it fast, hold the reaction.
- Pip never speaks. The narrator tells, Pip acts. No lip-sync.
- Parts never move in unison: head leads, arms follow, crowds are offset.
- Every scene keeps some life going: idle breath, blinks, a background extra, drifting dust or leaves.

## Cast

- **Pip**: teal pebble, mustard scarf. The "you" of the story. In almost every shot.
- **Momo**: coral, round glasses, cream shawl. The calm neighbour. Shots 9, 18, 29, 35, 36.
- **Extras**: the same rig with different colours and one accessory each (cap, bag, apron, white coat, headphones):
  barista, shopkeeper, bank teller, news anchor, nurse, doctor, courier, engineer, grandmother, creator, kids, crowd.

## Action library (rig moves to build once, reused everywhere)

## Act 1: The day it all stopped (0:00–1:20)

**S01 · HOME bedroom, dawn · 001–003 · 14 s** · camera: slow push-in, then pull-out to the window
- 001: Pip asleep under the blanket. Alarm buzz. An arm flops out, finds the phone, holds it over the face. A blue glow with a spinning circle lights the face.
- 002: *ding.* Pip sits bolt upright, eyes wide, brows jump. Taps the screen three times, fast.
- 003: Pip pads to the window. Camera pulls back: across the street, neighbours in every window hold their phones up, each at a different moment.

**S02 · BOARD · 004–007 · 25 s**
- 004: Paper globe at night, turning slowly. Glowing threads between cities snap one after another and the lights dim.
- 005: Pip slides in sitting on a calendar page, chin on hand. A question-mark cloud pops up.
- 006: Pip points. Icons pop in on each word, with a small bounce: light bulb, radio, TV, corded phone.
- 007: A row of devices slides in (laptop, phone, card machine, smart speaker). Screens go dark left to right and sleep puffs rise. Pip shrugs.

**S03 · HOME kitchen + living room, morning · 008–010 · 13 s**
- 008: Sunrise. Toast pops. Pip walks in and glances at the dark phone on the table.
- 009: Pip crouches behind the sofa, pulls the router plug, plugs it back in. The lights blink.
- 010: A second time, faster. Third time: Pip grabs the router with both arms and shakes it. Steam puffs. *boing.*

**S04 · BUILDING, day · 011 · 5 s** · camera: pan left
- 011: Every window shows a different pebble holding a router up, each shaking it at its own rhythm.

**S05 · HOME living room · 012–013 · 10 s**
- 012: Pip turns the big knob on the retro radio. Sound-wave strips ripple out.
- 013: Cut to the old TV: a news anchor in front of a world map, mouth moving, worried brows. Pip sinks slowly onto the sofa.

**S06 · BOARD, seabed · 014–016 · 14 s** · camera: drop from the ocean surface to the seabed
- 014: Cables on the seabed with light pulses running through them. Seaweed sways.
- 015: Inside a see-through cable, an envelope, a coin and a play button ride past as streaks.
- 016: The pulses stop and the cable dims. A small fish swims up, pokes it, swims off.

## Act 2: Chaos on the streets (1:20–1:46)

**S07 · STREET, morning rush · 017–019 · 14 s**
- 017: Cars stopped every which way. Drivers climb out and look around. *thud.*
- 018: At the station gates, commuters bunch up waving phones at closed gates.
- 019: Pip in the middle of the pavement, phone held to the sky, spins on the spot. *dizzy.*

**S08 · BOARD · 020 · 7 s**
- 020: A cheerful satellite beams down from the top. Below it, a phone shows an empty grey grid with one blue dot. Pip looks from one to the other.

**S09 · STREET · 021 · 5 s**
- 021: Pip shuffles up to Momo, who is reading a newspaper, and gives a small wave. Momo points down the street. Pip nods and walks off. *(Momo's first appearance.)*

## Act 3: Cash is king (1:46–2:18)

**S10 · STREET café window counter · 022–024 · 16 s**
- 022: Pip holds a card to the card machine. Red flash, buzz. The barista shrugs.
- 023: Close-up on the card machine: dark screen, red glow, a few coins.
- 024: The shopkeeper writes in a paper ledger, nods at a small coin purchase, shakes their head at a big one.

**S11 · STREET bank · 025–027 · 17 s** · camera: pan left along the queue
- 025: A long queue snakes to the cash machine. Shuffle, wait, shuffle.
- 026: The cash machine screen goes dark. The whole queue turns and shuffles to the bank door, where a nervous teller peeks out.
- 027: Pip comes out hugging a few bills. Sparkles. Every eye in the queue follows the money.

## Act 4: Work, shops and flights (2:18–3:23)

**S12 · HOME desk · 028 · 5 s**
- 028: Pip at the laptop, video-call grid frozen. Taps a key, sighs, slumps.

**S13 · BOARD · 029–033 · 31 s**
- 029: Warehouse vignette: the conveyor stops, parcels pile up, workers sit down on boxes.
- 030: A giant pointer hand freezes above a glowing button. Dust drifts.
- 031: Airport vignette: the departure board goes blank and travellers pile up with suitcases.
- 032: A wall of screens flips to solid blue, one by one. Staff hold their heads. A small "2024" tag.
- 033: Pip holds a tiny desk calendar. Behind Pip, a tower of calendar pages grows up and out of frame.

**S14 · HOSPITAL · 034–036 · 14 s**
- 034: A doctor walks the corridor with confidence and a small nod.
- 035: A nurse frowns at the dark computer and taps it.
- 036: The nurse runs past with a stack of folders, pages flying. *whoosh.*

**S15 · BUILDING, dusk → night · 037–038 · 9 s**
- 037: The light changes from day to dusk. Streets go quiet.
- 038: Windows glow warm. Inside: a family plays a board game, someone reads, two chat by a lamp.

**S16 · HOME bedroom, night · 039 · 7 s**
- 039: Pip in bed reaches for the phone, holds it a beat, puts it face down. Stares at the ceiling. Blink.

## Act 5: Week one (3:23–5:14)

**S17 · SHOP · 040–045 · 26 s**
- 040: Wall calendar pages flip, one to seven. *riser.*
- 041: Half-empty shelves. A shopper with an empty basket looks left, right.
- 042: The manager studies a wall map of farm → truck → shop. The dotted line is torn in the middle.
- 043: At the loading door, a driver holds a huge paper map that covers the whole windscreen.
- 044: Back office: a dusty fax machine wakes up and spits out paper. The manager jumps.
- 045: Pip pops in from the side holding the fax high like a trophy. *poof.*

**S18 · SQUARE + BOARD · 046–050 · 27 s**
- 046: (SQUARE) The crowd whispers ear to ear. Scribbled speech bubbles pop up.
- 047: Three gossipers. Thought bubbles pop in turn: a calendar, a bank with a padlock, a flying saucer.
- 048: (BOARD) Radio, TV and newspaper icons. Printing-press rollers spin out pages.
- 049: (SQUARE) The newsstand is mobbed. Two hands grab the last paper, and the vendor beams.
- 050: Pip and Momo read the notice board, heads tilting at the same notice.

**S19 · HOME window, rain · 051 · 4 s**
- 051: Pip holds a framed photo of a faraway friend. Rain runs down the window.

**S20 · BOARD · 052 · 7 s**
- 052: A cell tower on a hill. Signal rings travel out and fizzle into sparks halfway.

**S21 · SQUARE · 053–055 · 16 s**
- 053: A patient queue at the red payphone. The front pebble talks, everyone else waits.
- 054: The post office doors burst open with a flood of envelopes.
- 055: Mail trucks line up out front. Through the window, sorting belts carry envelopes.

**S22 · HOME desk · 056 · 7 s**
- 056: Pip writes, crosses out (*scrape*), crumples, tosses. Paper balls pile up. Then one finished letter, held up proudly.

## Act 6: Month one, the new normal (5:14–6:28)

**S23 · STREET, sunny · 057–059 · 16 s**

- 058: Through the bank window, clerks stamp paper forms. Thunk, thunk.

- 069: The map shop window, faces pressed to the glass.

## Act 7: Month six, getting used to it (6:28–7:31)

**S27 · PARK, autumn · 070–071 · 14 s**
- 070: Pip on a bench with a paperback. Birds hop closer.
- 071: Pip lies back on the grass, eyes closed, slow calm breaths. Leaves drift down.

**S28 · HOME drawer · 072 · 5 s**
- 072: A phone in a drawer, dusty, a little cobweb. The drawer slides shut.

**S29 · STREET, street fair · 073–075 · 18 s**
- 073: Bunting and stalls. Neighbours swap books and vegetables. Kids play hopscotch.
- 074: Pip and Momo crank a copy machine on the stoop, stack the sheets, push them through doors.

**S30 · BOARD · 076–080 · 25 s**
- 076: Grey clouds roll in. A paper arrow bends downward.
- 077: A cargo ship waits at port. Containers stacked, cranes still.
- 078: A forecaster squints at a paper weather chart and turns it upside down.
- 079: A job board, and a long line of pebbles reading it.
- 080: The paper globe, now dotted with small warm towns, with no lines between them.

## Act 8: Day 365 (7:31–8:04)

**S34 · BOARD · 087–088 · 14 s**

**S35 · BUILDING, day · 089 · 6 s**

**S36 · PARK rooftop → night · 090–091 · 10 s**

**S37 · HOME, close-up · 092 · 8 s**

**S38 · VOID · 093 · 7 s**
