# YouTube monetization: rules for this channel

Researched October 2026 from YouTube's Help Center. Re-check the official pages
before applying; YouTube changes these often.

- [YouTube Partner Program overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- [Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)

## 1. Eligibility (official)

Required before applying:

- Follow the channel monetization policies (section 2).
- Live in a country/region where YPP is available.
- No active Community Guidelines strikes.
- 2-Step Verification on the Google account.
- "Advanced features" access on YouTube (verify the channel, e.g. by phone).
- One active AdSense for YouTube account. Create it inside YouTube Studio, not on adsense.google.com.

Thresholds for ad revenue (either route):

| Route | Subscribers | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form | 1,000 | 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 12 months |
| Shorts | 1,000 | 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days |

- Shorts watch time does **not** count toward the 4,000 hours.
- Private, unlisted, deleted videos and paid ad-campaign views don't count.
- A lower "earlier access" tier exists for fan funding and Shopping. Third-party
  guides list 500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts
  views; we didn't confirm these numbers on YouTube's page.

Process:

- Apply in YouTube Studio → Earn. Accept the base terms and link AdSense.
- Automated systems plus human reviewers check the channel as a whole. A decision
  typically takes about 1 month.
- If rejected, appeal within 21 days, or re-apply after 30 days. If rejected again,
  re-apply after 90 days.
- Monetization may be turned off if the channel posts nothing for 6 months.
- **Starting February 1, 2027** YPP terms change. Accept the updated terms in
  YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027. Some blogs claim higher thresholds for new
  applicants from that date; that isn't stated on YouTube's page, so check it.

## 2. What reviewers check

Reviewers can't watch everything, so they focus on the channel's:

- main theme
- most viewed videos
- newest videos
- videos with the most watch time
- titles, thumbnails, descriptions
- "About" section

Content must be **original and authentic**: "not mass-produced, generic,
repetitive, or manipulative", and "made for the enjoyment or education of viewers,
rather than for the sole purpose of getting views".

### Policies that matter most for an AI-assisted, motion-graphics channel

**Inauthentic content: generic or repetitive** (renamed July 2025, clarified July 16, 2026)

Allowed:

- the same intro and outro when the bulk of each video differs
- a series format where each video has a distinct storyline, focus or concept

Not monetizable:

- "Similar or repetitive content with low educational value, commentary,
  narratives, or minimal variation across videos"
- "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no
  narrative, commentary, or educational value"
- "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the
  impression of mass production without adding the creator's original, authentic
  insights or perspective"

YouTube's trust and safety lead added that tutorials can fall under this when they
only reproduce what's already common on the platform.

**AI personas related to sensitive topics** (new July 2026)

- Channels "that use AI-generated personas to deliver information on sensitive
  topics… any content that presents itself as a human expert providing advice to
  viewers on topics such as health, legal issues, finances, or politics" can't monetize.
- YouTube's examples include "AI-generated podcast hosts offering financial
  guidance, investment tips, or wealth management advice".

**Unsatisfying or off-putting content**

- Not allowed: emotionally manipulative formulas, shock-only content, unrelated AI
  clips stitched together, and fake realistic events (e.g. a fake celebrity death).
- Allowed: "using AI to edit your video scripts or generate a unique background
  visual" in service of "a unique, well-researched, or creative narrative".

**Reused content**

- Not allowed: content "that exclusively features readings of other materials you
  did not originally create, like text from websites or news feeds".
- Not allowed: compilations and lightly edited downloads.
- Applies to the whole channel, even with permission from the original creator.

**Creator integrity**

- No fake engagement: bought views, subscribers or watch time.
- No new channels to dodge a suspension. That can terminate all your channels.

## 3. AI disclosure ("AI use" setting at upload)

Must disclose **realistic** AI content:

- a real person appearing to say or do something they didn't
- altered footage of real events or places
- realistic scenes that didn't happen
- **AI-generated music** that is the main focus of the video

No disclosure needed:

- clearly non-realistic or animated content
- AI help with scripts, outlines, titles, thumbnails, infographics or captions
- **cloning your own voice for voice-overs or dubs**

Disclosure "alone won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn
money". When in doubt, disclose. YouTube can also add the label itself.

## 4. What this means for our pipeline

| Our tool | Risk | Rule we follow |
|---|---|---|
| HyperFrames templates | Generic/repetitive content | A reusable intro, outro and brand look is fine. Each video needs its own script, research, structure and visuals. Never swap only the title or numbers. |
| Kokoro / AI voice | AI-persona rule on finance/health/legal topics; viewer drop-off | No fake named "expert" host and no AI avatar giving advice. Narrate explanations, not personal advice. **Best option: the owner's own voice, or a clone of it (no disclosure needed), dubbed into other languages.** |
| MusicGen | Disclosure for AI music | Prefer the YouTube Audio Library or licensed tracks. Disclose if the AI music is prominent. |
| Bundled SFX, own motion graphics | Low | Fine. |
| Whisper captions | None | Fine. Captions are production help. |
| News / web sources | Reused content | Never just read an article. Add our own analysis, data visuals and comparisons, and cite sources on screen. |

## 5. Checklist before applying to YPP

- [ ] Channel has a clear theme, with an "About" section describing who makes it and why.
- [ ] Every video has an original script with our own analysis or perspective.
- [ ] Visual structure varies between videos (not one template with different text).
- [ ] No AI host or persona presenting as an expert on money, health, law or politics.
- [ ] Finance videos are educational. They show sources and say "not financial advice".
- [ ] Music is from the Audio Library or licensed, and any AI music is disclosed.
- [ ] "AI use" setting answered honestly on every upload.
- [ ] No bought views or subscribers, and no engagement pods.
- [ ] 2-Step Verification and advanced features on. AdSense created in YouTube Studio.
- [ ] Upload regularly; a 6-month gap can switch monetization off.
